# PWA Print Materials Specifications

This document outlines comprehensive print materials for the PWA rollout campaign.

## Overview

Three types of print materials are required:
1. **A3 Clinic Posters** (10 copies) - High-traffic area displays
2. **A5 Quick-Start Cards** (100 copies) - Training handouts
3. **ID Badge Stickers** (60 copies) - Personal reference cards

**Budget**: $400 total
**Timeline**: Week 1-1.5 of rollout preparation
**Print Quality**: Professional color printing

---

## Material 1: A3 Clinic Posters

### Specifications
- **Size**: A3 (297mm × 420mm / 11.7" × 16.5")
- **Orientation**: Portrait
- **Quantity**: 10 copies
- **Print**: Full color, glossy or matte finish
- **Material**: 200gsm cardstock (durable)
- **Estimated cost**: $80-100 (10 copies @ $8-10 each)

### Design Requirements

#### Layout Structure

**Header Section** (Top 20%)

## Production Workflow

### Step 1: Design (3-4 days)
**Tools**:
- **Adobe Illustrator** (vector graphics, professional)
- **Canva Pro** (user-friendly templates)
- **Figma** (collaborative design)

**Tasks**:
1. Create A3 poster design (Vietnamese version)
2. Create A5 quick-start card (front + back, Vietnamese + English)
3. Create ID badge sticker (3 versions, choose best)
4. Get internal approval from management
5. Incorporate feedback and finalize designs

### Step 2: Pre-Press Preparation (1 day)
**Tasks**:
1. Convert designs to CMYK color mode
2. Check resolution (300 DPI for posters/cards, 600 DPI for stickers)
3. Add bleed and crop marks
4. Embed all fonts
5. Export to PDF (PDF/X-1a standard for professional printing)
6. Create print-ready files:
   - `poster-a3-clinic-viet-uc.pdf`
   - `quickstart-card-a5-front.pdf`
   - `quickstart-card-a5-back.pdf`
   - `id-badge-sticker-50mm.pdf`

### Step 3: Printing (2-3 days)
**Options**:

**Option A: Local Print Shop** (Recommended for Vietnam)
- Find local print shop in Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi
- Request quote for quantities specified
- Provide print-ready PDFs
- Request sample/proof before full print run
- Estimated turnaround: 2-3 days

**Option B: Online Printing Service**
- **Vistaprint** (ships to Vietnam, may have delays)
- **Moo.com** (high quality, premium pricing)
- **Local Vietnamese print services**:
  - In Kỹ Thuật Số (Ho Chi Minh City)
  - VietInAnh (Hanoi)
  - FastPrint.vn (online service)

**Option C: Odoo-Based Printing** (if available)
- Some Odoo partners offer print services
- Can integrate with system for automated reordering

**Printing Checklist**:
- [ ] Request printed proof/sample (1 copy of each material)
- [ ] Check colors match design (especially purple #875A7B)
- [ ] Verify QR codes scan correctly
- [ ] Check text legibility (especially small text on stickers)
- [ ] Approve full print run after proof approval
- [ ] Confirm delivery date

### Step 4: Quality Control (1 day)
**Upon receiving printed materials**:
1. **Visual inspection**:
   - Colors accurate?
   - Text sharp and readable?
   - QR codes print clearly?
   - No smudges or printing errors?

2. **Functional testing**:
   - Scan QR codes with multiple devices (iPhone, Android)
   - Verify QR codes link to correct URLs
   - Test sticker adhesive (peel and stick on sample ID badge)

3. **Count verification**:
   - 10 A3 posters ✓
   - 100 A5 cards (50 sheets, double-sided) ✓
   - 60 ID badge stickers ✓

4. **Packaging**:
   - Posters: Rolled in tube or flat in portfolio
   - Cards: Bundled in sets of 10
   - Stickers: On backing sheet (10-15 stickers per sheet)

### Step 5: Distribution (1 day)
**Prepare distribution kits**:
- **Training Kit** (for each training session):
  - 20 A5 quick-start cards
  - 10 ID badge stickers
  - 1 A3 poster (if needed for training room)

- **Clinic Kit** (for each clinic location):
  - 2-3 A3 posters (reception, break room, training room)
  - 10 A5 quick-start cards
  - 5 ID badge stickers

**Distribution schedule**:
- Week 1.5: Deliver to pilot group (5-10 users)
- Week 2: Distribute to training locations
- Week 3-4: Full field staff rollout
- Week 5: Admin staff rollout

Before sending to printer:

- [ ] All designs finalized and approved

- [ ] High resolution (300 DPI posters/cards, 600 DPI stickers)
- [ ] Fonts embedded or outlined

- [ ] Safe zone marked (10mm margin)
- [ ] PDF/X-1a format exported

**QR Codes**:
- [ ] QR codes generated at high resolution

- [ ] Contact information accurate (phone, email, Zalo, website)

**Colors**:
- [ ] Brand colors accurate (#875A7B purple)

**Layout**:
- [ ] Visual hierarchy clear (most important info prominent)

- [ ] Spacing balanced (not cramped)
- [ ] Images and icons high quality (not pixelated)

## Environmental Considerations

### Eco-Friendly Options (Optional)
- **Recycled paper**: 170-200gsm recycled cardstock (adds $20-30)
- **Soy-based inks**: Environmentally friendly printing (may not be available in Vietnam)
- **FSC-certified paper**: Sustainably sourced (adds $10-20)

### Waste Reduction
- **Print exactly what's needed**: Avoid overprinting
- **Digital-first approach**: Print materials supplement (not replace) digital guides
- **Reusable materials**: Laminated posters can be wiped clean and reused
- **Repurposing**: Use outdated materials as scratch paper (if info becomes obsolete)

---

## Reorder Strategy

### When to Reorder
- **A3 Posters**: Rarely (10 copies should last 1-2 years)
- **A5 Cards**: When stock drops below 20 (quarterly check)
- **ID Badge Stickers**: When stock drops below 10 (monthly check)

### Reorder Quantities
- **Small reorder**: 25 A5 cards, 15 stickers (~$80)
- **Large reorder**: 100 A5 cards, 50 stickers (~$300)

### Version Control
- Include version number and date on all materials
- Update designs when:
  - URLs change
  - Contact information updates
  - Major UI changes in app
  - New features require updated instructions
- Archive old versions (don't discard unsold stock immediately - may still be usable)

## Success Metrics

Track effectiveness of print materials:
- **QR code scans**: Track UTM campaigns in analytics
  - `clinic_poster` campaign: How many scans from posters?
  - `training_session` campaign: Scans from quick-start cards?
  - `id_badge` campaign: Scans from stickers?

- **Feedback surveys**:
  - "Did you use the printed installation guide?" (Yes/No)
  - "Was the printed guide helpful?" (1-5 scale)
  - "Which material was most useful?" (Poster/Card/Sticker/None)

- **Installation success rate**:
  - Compare users who received print materials vs. digital-only
  - Target: >90% installation success with print materials

- **Support ticket reduction**:
  - Fewer "how to install" questions after distributing materials
  - Target: <5% support tickets related to installation
