# Appendix — Glossary, Statuses & Roles

This appendix is your quick-reference guide. Whenever you see a status label, a colour, or a role name in the CMS and you are not sure what it means, come back here. Keep it open in a second tab while you learn the system.

## Statuses at a Glance

These are the labels you will see most often on records throughout the CMS. Each one tells you where a record is in its journey.

### Booking States

A booking moves through these states from the moment it is created until it is finalised. The state controls what you can do with the booking next.

**What you see on this screen:**

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Created, but not yet confirmed |
| Confirmed / Booked | Client confirmed, and the booking has a quote |
| Assigned | A staff member has been assigned to the booking |
| In Progress | The service is currently running |
| Completed | The service is done |
| Completed-Pending-Invoice | Done by part-time staff; the office still needs to invoice it |
| Cancelled | The booking was cancelled |
| Closed | Finalised and locked |

**Note:** "Completed-Pending-Invoice" is not the same as "Completed." It means the service is finished but billing is still outstanding, so the office must raise the invoice before the booking can be closed.

### Contact Statuses

A contact status tells you what kind of relationship you have with a person or lead, so you know who needs follow-up and who has already booked.

**What you see on this screen:**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active / Initial Contact | A new or active contact |
| Lead | A contact to follow up with |
| Booking | This contact has booked a service |
| Lost Booking | A booking opportunity that did not convert |
| Spam | Junk or unwanted contact |

### Assignment Statuses

An assignment status follows a single staff member's progress on a job, from the moment they are assigned to the moment they finish.

**What you see on this screen:**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Assigned | The nurse has been assigned to the job |
| Confirmed | The nurse has accepted the job |
| In Progress | The nurse has started the job |
| Completed | The nurse has finished the job |
| Cancelled | The assignment was cancelled |

**Note:** An assignment normally flows Assigned → Confirmed → In Progress → Completed. It can be Cancelled at any point.

### Payment Statuses

A payment status tells you where the money is, which is especially important when nurses collect cash in the field.

**What you see on this screen:**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Collected | Payment has been taken |
| Pending Delivery | Cash is with the nurse, not yet handed in at the office |
| Delivered / Reconciled | Cash has reached the office and the books match |
| Failed / Refunded | The payment failed or was refunded |

**Warning:** A status of "Pending Delivery" means real cash is still out with a nurse. It is not money in the office until it shows as "Delivered / Reconciled."

## Roles & Visibility

Your role decides which screens and records you can see. This keeps each person focused on the records that belong to them.

### Who Sees What

**What you see on this screen:**

| Role | What they see |
| --- | --- |
| Nurse | Only the bookings they are assigned to, plus clients in their own catchment area |
| Operations Manager | All bookings and clients in their catchment |
| Finance | The Finance screens |
| Owner / Administrator | Everything |

**Note:** A user can be given more than one role. Their access is the combination of all their roles, so someone who is both Finance and Operations Manager sees both sets of screens.

## Handy Tips for New Users

These small habits will make day-to-day work in the CMS much faster.

- **Change every figure at once:** use the period filter on a dashboard to update all the figures on that dashboard together.
- **See the records behind a number:** click any KPI card to open the list of records that make up that number.
- **Go back a step:** use the breadcrumb in the top-left corner to return to the previous screen.
- **Check a record's history:** the chatter on a record logs its full history, so you can see what happened and when.
- **Ask for help:** the AI Coach in the bottom-right corner can answer your questions.

**Tip:** When a number on a dashboard looks surprising, click the KPI card to drill into the actual records behind it before raising a concern. The detail almost always explains the figure.
