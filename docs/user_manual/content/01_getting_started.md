# Getting Started

Welcome to the Viet UC CMS. This chapter explains what the system is for, how to sign in, and the handful of patterns that work the same way on every screen. Once you understand the ideas here, the rest of the manual will feel familiar.

## What Viet UC CMS Is For

Viet UC CMS is the home-healthcare operations platform for the Viet Uc clinic. You use it to run the day-to-day work of caring for patients in their homes, all in one place. It manages:

- **Contacts and leads** — people who enquire about your services.
- **Clients and patients** — the people you care for.
- **Bookings** — home-visit appointments.
- **Staff and assignments** — who is going to which visit.
- **Invoicing and payments** — billing for the care you provide.
- **A separate Nurse Mobile App** — used by nurses out in the field.

**Note:** What you can see and do depends on your assigned role. For example, a nurse sees only the bookings they are assigned to and only clients in their catchment area; Finance staff see the Finance screens; an admin or owner sees everything. See the Appendix for the full role and visibility summary.

## Logging In

This is how you open the system and reach your starting screen.

**How to use it:**

1. Open a web browser and go to **care.biztinct.com**.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Sign in. After login you land on a dashboard — typically the **Operations Center**, depending on your role.

**Tip:** The dashboard you see first is chosen for your role, so two different staff members may land on different screens. That is normal.

## Finding Your Way Around

This section explains the two parts of the screen you will use constantly: the left sidebar (for navigation) and the main area (where each screen opens).

![The Operations Center dashboard, showing the left sidebar, the period filter row, the KPI cards, and the green refresh button.](IMG:ops_01_dashboard.png)

### The Left Sidebar

The left sidebar is your main navigation. It stays available so you can move between screens at any time.

**What you see on this screen:**

- The **Viet Uc logo / company** at the top.
- Four **collapsible sections**: **CRM**, **OPERATIONS MANAGER**, **FINANCE**, and **ADMIN**.
- The individual menu items inside each section.

**How to use it:**

1. Click a section heading (CRM, Operations Manager, Finance, or Admin) to expand or collapse it and reveal its items.
2. Click any item to open that screen in the main area to the right.

**Note:** You may not see every section. The sidebar shows only the areas your role gives you access to.

### The Top Bar

The top bar runs across the top-right of every screen and gives you quick tools no matter where you are.

**What you see on this screen:**

- A **search** box.
- A **chat / messages** counter.
- An **activities** counter.
- The **company name**.
- Your **user menu**.

## Patterns You Only Need to Learn Once

These behaviors work the same way everywhere in the system. Learn them here and you will recognise them on every screen.

### The Period Filter

Every dashboard has a period filter row across the top. It controls the time range that all the numbers on the page are based on.

**What you see on this screen:**

- A row of choices: **Today**, **This Week**, **This Month**, **All Dates**, and **Custom**.

**How to use it:**

1. Click a period (for example, **This Week**) to re-scope every figure on the page to that time range.
2. Choose **Custom** to set your own date range.

**Note:** Changing the period updates *every* figure on the page at once, not just one card.

### KPI Cards and Drill-Down

KPI cards are the summary tiles on a dashboard, each showing a key number. They are not just for reading — they are clickable.

**How to use it:**

1. Click a KPI card.
2. The system opens the underlying filtered list — the records behind that number. This is called a **drill-down**.

**Tip:** Drill-down is the fastest way to go from "how many" to "exactly which ones." If a number looks surprising, click it to see what is inside.

### Lists and Forms

Most screens show a **list** of records, and each list opens into a detailed **form**.

**How to use it:**

1. On a list, use the **search** box or the **filter tabs** to narrow down what you see.
2. Click a row to open its detailed record (the **form**).
3. To go back, use the **breadcrumb** at the top-left of the screen.

### The Chatter on Forms

Every form includes a "chatter" panel that keeps a history of the record.

- It records a **log of activity and history** for that record.
- It lets you **schedule follow-ups** so nothing is forgotten.

### The Refresh Button

When you want the latest figures, use the green refresh button.

**How to use it:**

1. Click the green **Refresh** button.
2. The current dashboard reloads its data.

**Tip:** If you have just made a change elsewhere and want to confirm it shows up, click Refresh rather than reloading the whole browser.

### The AI Coach

A floating **AI Coach** button sits in the bottom-right corner of the screen.

**How to use it:**

1. Click the **AI Coach** button at any time.
2. It offers in-app help to guide you while you work.

**Tip:** If you get stuck on a screen, the AI Coach is the quickest place to ask for help without leaving what you are doing.
