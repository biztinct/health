# CRM — Contacts & Leads

The CRM section is where you manage enquiries — the people who call, message, or reach out to the clinic — and guide each one along their journey toward a confirmed booking. This chapter covers the three items in this section: the **Dashboard**, **Contacts**, and **Activities**.

## Overview

Every enquiry becomes a **contact**. As you work with that person, their status moves through a simple lifecycle:

- **Active (Initial Contact)** — a new enquiry has just come in.
- **Lead** — you have marked them for future follow-up.
- **Booking** — a service has been booked for them.
- **Lost Booking** — a booking fell through.
- **Spam** — the enquiry is junk and is flagged to ignore.

The Dashboard gives you the big picture, Contacts is where you do the day-to-day work, and Activities is your shared follow-up to-do board.

## Dashboard

### CRM Dashboard

The CRM Dashboard is your at-a-glance command center. Use it at the start of your shift to see how many enquiries came in, what still needs follow-up, and how the team is doing on conversions.

![The CRM Center dashboard with KPI cards, charts, and activity feeds](IMG:crm_01_dashboard.png)

**What you see on this screen**

- A header titled **"CRM Center — Sales & Contact Management"**.
- A **period filter** at the top — choose **Today**, **Week**, **Month**, **All**, or **Custom** — plus a **refresh** button to reload the latest numbers.
- A row of **KPI cards**, each one clickable:
  - **Contacts Today** — enquiries received today.
  - **Pending Follow-ups** — leads that are waiting for you to follow up.
  - **Active Leads** — leads currently in progress.
  - **Bookings This Week** — bookings created this week.
  - **Conversion Rate** — your conversion rate this month.
  - **Spam Rate** — the share of enquiries marked as spam.
- A second strip of stats: **New This Month**, **Converted**, **Lost**, and **Active Pipeline**.
- A **"Contact Pipeline"** donut chart breaking contacts down by status (Booking / Lead / Lost Booking / Spam).
- A **"Source Channels"** bar chart showing where your contacts came from.
- Three feed panels:
  - **Recent Contacts** — each entry shows a status dot, the name, the channel, and the time.
  - **Upcoming Bookings** — the bookings coming up next.
  - **Contact Status** — a bar per status with its count.

**How to use it**

1. Choose a time range in the **period filter** (Today / Week / Month / All / Custom). The whole dashboard updates to that range.
2. Click **refresh** at any time to pull the latest figures.
3. Click any **KPI card** to drill straight into a filtered list of the matching contacts or bookings.
4. In the **Contact Pipeline** donut, click a segment (for example, **Lead**) to open the contacts filtered to that status.
5. In **Recent Contacts**, click a row to open that contact, or click **View All** to open the full contacts list.
6. In **Upcoming Bookings**, click an entry to open that booking.

**Tip:** Every KPI card and every chart segment is a shortcut. If a number looks surprising, click it to see exactly which contacts it represents.

## Contacts

### Contacts

Contacts is your working list of every enquiry. From here you can see each person's status, take quick action on them, and open their full record. This is where most of your day-to-day CRM work happens.

![The Contacts list with status tabs and quick action buttons on each row](IMG:crm_02_contacts_list.png)

**What you see on this screen**

- Tabs to filter the list: **All**, **Active**, **Leads**, **Bookings**, **Lost**, and **Spam**.
- **Date filters** to narrow the list by when contacts came in.
- On each row, a set of **quick action buttons**: **Book**, **Log Activity**, **Escalate**, and **Spam**.

**How to use it**

1. Click a tab (for example, **Leads**) to show only contacts with that status.
2. Use the **date filters** to focus on a specific time period.
3. On any row, use the quick action buttons to act without opening the contact (see the button reference below).
4. Click a contact's row to open its full **contact form**.

### Contact form

Opening a contact shows its full form, with all the key action buttons in the header. Use this when you want to work a single enquiry in detail — book them in, log a follow-up, escalate, or close them out.

![The contact form with action buttons in the header](IMG:crm_02b_contact_form.png)

**What you see on this screen**

- The contact's details.
- A header with the key action buttons: **Book**, **Log Activity**, **Log as Lead**, **Escalate**, **Spam**, and (when the contact is already a booking) **Booking Lost**.

**How to use it**

1. To turn the enquiry into a booking, click **Book**. The **Quick Booking** wizard opens. It searches existing clients so you don't create a duplicate — confirm the matching client or create a new one, then fill in the **service type**, **facility**, **date/time**, and **duration**. Click **Create Booking** to create the booking; the contact's status changes to **booking**.
2. To schedule a follow-up, click **Log Activity** and set up a call or meeting on the contact.
3. To keep the enquiry for later, click **Log as Lead**. The status changes to **lead**.
4. To hand the contact to a specialist, click **Escalate**. The contact is routed to the Head Nurse, OM, or Duty Doctor.
5. If the enquiry is junk, click **Spam**. The contact is marked as spam and its phone number is flagged for the future.
6. If a booking fell through, click **Booking Lost** (shown only when the status is **booking**) and record the lost-booking reason.

**Button reference**

| Button | What it does | Resulting status |
|---|---|---|
| Book | Opens the Quick Booking wizard; find/confirm the client, fill service type, facility, date-time, duration, then Create Booking | booking |
| Log Activity | Schedules a follow-up activity (call/meeting) on the contact | (unchanged) |
| Log as Lead | Keeps the contact for future follow-up | lead |
| Escalate | Routes the contact to a specialist (Head Nurse / OM / Duty Doctor) | (unchanged) |
| Spam | Marks the contact as spam and flags the phone number | spam |
| Booking Lost | Records a lost-booking reason (only when status is booking) | lost_booking |

**Note:** The **Book** wizard checks for existing clients before creating a new one. Always confirm a suggested match when it appears, so you avoid duplicate client records.

**Tip:** The quick action buttons on the list rows (**Book**, **Log Activity**, **Escalate**, **Spam**) do the same thing as the matching buttons on the contact form — use whichever is faster for what you're doing.

## Activities

### Activities

The Activities board is the team's shared follow-up to-do list. Every call, meeting, email, or task you schedule on a contact shows up here, so nothing slips through the cracks.

![The Activities board grouped by activity type](IMG:crm_03_activities.png)

**What you see on this screen**

- A board of scheduled activities grouped by type: **To-Do**, **Email**, **Call**, **Meeting**, and **Document**.
- A **Type filter** to show only one kind of activity.
- A **Date filter**: **All Dates**, **Today**, **This Week**, or **This Month**.
- A **+ Schedule activity** button.

**How to use it**

1. Use the **Type filter** to focus on a single activity type (for example, only **Call**).
2. Use the **Date filter** to show activities due **Today**, **This Week**, **This Month**, or across **All Dates**.
3. Click **+ Schedule activity** to open the picker, choose the contact, and schedule the activity.

**Tip:** Check this board regularly — it's how the whole team keeps track of who needs a follow-up and when.
