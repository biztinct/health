Root cause of the six (diagnosed, NOT fixed — out of sanction):
they are the §5.50/§5.95 family, live deployment data leaking into fixtures.
`biz_bi/tests/common.py` builds a dataset over **res.partner** and expects its
own three rows; this database has 77 partners carrying a latitude
(`sum = 897.31`). And `res_country.name` is jsonb —
`{"en_US": "Vietnam", "vi_VN": "Việt Nam"}` — so `TestRls`'s row rule, built
from `country_a.name` in the caller's language, matches no row in the silver
view and the restricted total comes back NULL instead of 30.0.

Security note, since three of the six are in the RLS suite: every one of them
fails **closed** (the restricted user sees `None`, never the unrestricted
60.0), so none of them is evidence of a leak.
