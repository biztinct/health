Reading it as the phase's own proof:

* `rows: 1000` is a PREVIEW — the wizard's `RECORDS_PREVIEW_LIMIT`. 1639 and
  1671 are the last-12-months preview (1,229 available, clamped to 1,000);
  1646 is the vi_VN drive with no date filter (1,271 available, clamped to
  1,000), which is why that banner reads "1.271" and why `meta.truncated`
  fired NATURALLY there — nothing had to be staged.
* `rows: 1229` on an `export` is the whole result set: the export route
  ignores the preview limit and re-runs at `biz_bi.export_row_cap`
  (`capped: false` because 1,229 < 20,000). Three exports: the button click
  on each drive, plus one byte-capture replay of the identical POST from the
  same authenticated session (the headless context does not persist
  downloads to disk).
* `rows: 1229` on a `query` right after a `dashboard_view` is the saved
  widget fetching at its `limit: 5000` and rendering 200 (§5.139's cap, whose
  overflow row the tile states out loud).
