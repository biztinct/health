RT-2 — QA fixtures created, and the fresh-cursor proof they are gone
====================================================================

§5.34 (verify the revert from a SEPARATE cursor) and §5.128 (an append-only
log keyed on `res.users` makes the persona undeletable until its rows go out
with a raw scoped DELETE — planned for BEFORE the persona was created).

Two throwaway personas were used, both cloned from the live `om` account
(uid 29), one per build:

    uid 8219  rt2_qa   (partner 19660)  first drive
    uid 8236  rt2_qa2  (partner 19686)  re-drive of 01/02/07/15/16 on the
                                        SHIPPED build, after 8219 was deleted

Both were Operations Manager role, `group_bi_creator` only. The service was
restarted after creating each of them and again after each `lang` flip
(§5.132 / §5.48 / new §5.141 — a write from a separate shell is invisible to
the running workers until then, and for `lang` a fresh LOGIN is not enough).

------------------------------

    bi.dashboard         611        ("RT2 QA Records Dashboard")
    bi.dashboard.widget  68, 72

The second persona created no chart, dashboard or widget: its drive stopped
at the step-3 preview (screens 15/16), which is why its cleanup reports
`widgets [] dashboards [] charts []`.

Temporary changes, all reverted:

    a. `res.users(8219).lang` set to `vi_VN` for the two-language pass and
       back to `en_US`; `res.users(8236).lang` set to `vi_VN`. Both users are
       now deleted entirely, so neither setting survives.
    b. NOTHING else. In particular the truncation banner did NOT have to be
       staged this time (RT-1 had to lower a constant for it): the wizard's
       render cap is 100 and every dataset on this deployment is larger, so
       the banner fires from a real envelope — and the vi_VN drive, with no
       date filter, also produced the genuine ENGINE truncation
       (`meta.truncated`, 1,000 fetched of 1,271). No deployed file was
       edited, no `ir.config_parameter` was written, no `ir.rule` was
       created outside the test transaction.

`bi.query.cache` was NOT touched: it is keyed on the request, carries no
`create_uid`/`write_uid` column at all, and is TTL-managed — there is nothing
to scope to a persona. (The first cleanup attempt tried to delete from it by
`create_uid`, got `UndefinedColumn`, and the whole transaction rolled back
uncommitted; the corrected script below is the one that ran.)
