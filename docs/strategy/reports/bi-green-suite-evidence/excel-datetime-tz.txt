on care.biztinct.com for the SAME report (Bookings & Service Orders,

Every one of the five appears in workbook 1 at its Sydney wall clock and in
workbook 2 at its Vietnamese wall clock. Before this phase both workbooks
would have carried the stored_utc column verbatim — seven hours early for
the Vietnamese users this deployment is for, and a whole DAY early for any
booking after 17:00 UTC.

Note on row 4 of each dump: the cell reads 15:59:59 / 18:59:59 rather than
16:00:00 / 19:00:00. That is this dumper reconstructing a float from
xlsxwriter's serial (46113.66666666666); Excel rounds the same serial to the
cell's yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm:ss format and shows the whole minute.

The cells are real Excel datetimes (number-formatted serials), not strings —
which is why a string-only reader sees nothing here at all, and why the new
test reads the numeric <v> of column B rather than sharedStrings.
