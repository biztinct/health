config_json exactly as written by `ReportWizard.buildConfigJson()`:

 {"sort": [], "limit": 500,
  "slots": {"x":      [{"field_id": 892}],
            "series": [],
            "values": [{"agg": "sum", "field_id": 650}]},
  "display": {},
  "filters": [{"op": "relative", "value": "last_12_months", "field_id": 593}],
  "version": 1,
  "chart_type": "bar"}

  * field 650 = "Total Price" (role measure, dataset 5 Bookings & Service
    Orders), field 892 = "Facility" (dimension), field 593 = "Scheduled
    Date" (date).
  * THE DATE-RANGE FORMAT the handover asked to be reported:
        {"field_id": <bi.field id>, "op": "relative", "value": "<range key>"}
    where <range key> is a key of `RELATIVE_RANGES`
    (biz_bi/static/src/core/range_labels.js — mirrored 1:1 by
    biz_bi/models/bi_query_engine.py:45-75). Here: `last_12_months`.
  * `x[0]` carries NO `grain` key, because the group-by is not a date and
    JSON.stringify drops the `undefined`. That asymmetry is pinned by
    `test_01_wizard_config_roundtrip`.
