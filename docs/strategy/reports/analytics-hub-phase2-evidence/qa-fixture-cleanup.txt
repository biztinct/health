How it was removed
------------------
`bi.audit.log` is append-only: its `unlink()` raises, and `user_id` is a
required Many2one, which PostgreSQL materialises as ON DELETE RESTRICT — so
the QA account cannot be deleted through the ORM until its rows are gone
(the same trap Phase 1 recorded). The BI rows were therefore removed with a
raw DELETE scoped to this one uid, in one transaction; the account itself
was then unlinked through the ORM.

  psql -d vietuat -c "
  BEGIN;
  DELETE FROM bi_dashboard_widget WHERE dashboard_id IN
      (SELECT id FROM bi_dashboard WHERE owner_id=7996);   -- DELETE 1
  DELETE FROM bi_dashboard WHERE owner_id=7996;            -- DELETE 1
  DELETE FROM bi_chart     WHERE owner_id=7996;            -- DELETE 1
  DELETE FROM bi_ai_log    WHERE user_id=7996;             -- DELETE 1
  DELETE FROM bi_audit_log WHERE user_id=7996;             -- DELETE 6
  COMMIT;"

  odoo-bin shell:  env['res.users'].browse(7996).unlink()
                   partner.unlink(); flush_all(); cr.commit()
  -> UNLINKING ahp2_qa_ops partner 19090
  -> DONE

No audit row belonging to any other user was touched.
