A01–A14 implementation design

Demo scope: carejiox, hhh and golden template. Vietnam production hosting remains the user’s responsibility. Internal authenticated clinician confirmation is distinct from CA signing. Government interfaces stay disabled until approved specifications and credentials exist.

Changes preserve historical seals and never backfill fictional consent acts. Evidence is generated after implementation and successful verification, under 02-evidence/<release>/<package>/, with capture time, environment, source commit, result and checksum. Client declarations remain unissued until all their applicable conditions are met.
