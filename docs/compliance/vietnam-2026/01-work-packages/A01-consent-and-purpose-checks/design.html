A01 — Consent and purpose checks

Design before implementation; accepted test evidence will be generated only after a fix is in place.

Components

health_consent, health_pwa, health_ai_coding, health_scribe, health_fhir_core

Requirement coverage

EV-02-2, EV-07-1, EV-07-2, EV-07-3, EV-11-4

Expected application exhibit

Consent record, purpose history, printable consent and processing-denial message.

Verification and negative cases

Witness grant, missing consent, withdrawal and queued/offline replay across backend, FHIR, PWA, AI and marketing. Verify permissible other-lawful-basis processing remains available.

Artifacts after implementation

A01-consent-exhibit.pdf; A01-purpose-cases.csv; A01-enforcement-results.json; tenant-configuration.json. Include notice version, exact event times and test identities.

Review and ongoing operation

Approve purpose/legal-basis map and consent wording. Reviewer checks every enabled path and tenant/template. Refresh when purposes, notices, callers or enforcement settings change.

History and migration

Do not invent legacy consent times, rewrite existing clinical seals, or record mock authority responses as real acceptance.

Deployment

carejiox, carejiox_template, hhh — demo environments

Dependencies and claim limits

Technical acceptance does not replace policy approval, government decisions or clinic obligations.

Implemented control scope

Consent records capture exact notice/version, actor and event time. Purpose lookup includes AI, support, analytics, training and audio recording. Immutable selected recipients bind AI transmission. Photo/audio upload gates run before storage. PWA and backend expose notice/recipient controls.

Remaining acceptance and external work

Approve notices, representative authority and lawful exceptions; arrange actual provider destinations before AI/speech use.
