A03 — Protected audit trail

Design before implementation; accepted test evidence will be generated only after a fix is in place.

Components

health_compliance, health_api_gateway

Requirement coverage

EV-01-4, EV-04-3, EV-04-5, EV-05-4

Expected application exhibit

Scoped audit search and export, with accurate actor and event sequence.

Verification and negative cases

Generate login/logout, clinical read/create/change/delete, support and API events; reconcile all expected events and proxy metadata. Attempt unauthorised alteration and retrieve archived logs.

Artifacts after implementation

A03-event-coverage.csv; audit-sample.ndjson; A03-tamper-and-retrieval.pdf; protected-store and retention configuration with access review.

Review and ongoing operation

Security reviewer witnesses coverage and protection against relevant administrator roles. Evidence archive configuration and ongoing log continuity; a fresh deployment cannot prove 12 elapsed months of retained history.

History and migration

Do not invent legacy consent times, rewrite existing clinical seals, or record mock authority responses as real acceptance.

Deployment

carejiox, carejiox_template, hhh — demo environments

Dependencies and claim limits

Technical acceptance does not replace policy approval, government decisions or clinic obligations.

Implemented control scope

Healthcare ORM read/fetch/create/write/delete, partner reads, clinical signing and attachment streaming emit structured actor/time/record/result events without content. AUTHPRIV syslog is independent of the request transaction.

Remaining acceptance and external work

Configure and witness log collection, retention, access and independent/off-host protection. Direct SQL, external systems and all export channels still require coverage review; local logs are not proof against infrastructure administrators.
