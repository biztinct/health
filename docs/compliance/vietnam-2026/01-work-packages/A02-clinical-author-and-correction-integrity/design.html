A02 — Clinical author and correction integrity

Design before implementation; accepted test evidence will be generated only after a fix is in place.

Components

health_emr, health_pwa

Requirement coverage

EV-05-1, EV-05-2, EV-05-3

Expected application exhibit

Finalized note, author/care/entry/sign times, rejected reassignment and linked addendum with mandatory reason.

Verification and negative cases

Attempt author/order relinking, content change and deletion/cascade through UI and APIs. Demonstrate original preservation, addendum export and offline identity attribution.

Artifacts after implementation

A02-note-addendum-exhibit.pdf; A02-mutation-results.json; original-and-addendum.json; SHA-256 manifest. Use synthetic clinical records.

Review and ongoing operation

Clinical reviewer approves late-entry/correction policy; security reviewer checks privileged access boundaries. Refresh after authoring, sync or schema changes.

History and migration

Do not invent legacy consent times, rewrite existing clinical seals, or record mock authority responses as real acceptance.

Deployment

carejiox, carejiox_template, hhh — demo environments

Dependencies and claim limits

Technical acceptance does not replace policy approval, government decisions or clinic obligations.

Implemented control scope

Authenticated finalization issues a v2 clinical seal over care time, correction reason, identity, audio and coding sidecars. RPC-created signatures and client-context bypasses are refused. Author/order/content lock after finalization; corrections use addenda. Existing v1 verification remains unchanged.

Remaining acceptance and external work

Approve clinician identity and supervisor permissions. Legacy missing event times remain missing.
