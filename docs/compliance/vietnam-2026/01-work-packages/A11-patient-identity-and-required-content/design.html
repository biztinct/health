A11 — Patient identity and required content

Design before implementation; accepted test evidence will be generated only after a fix is in place.

Components

health_fhir_adapter_vn, health_compliance

Requirement coverage

EV-09-2

Expected application exhibit

Verified identifier, duplicate alert, mandatory-field refusal and approved exception.

Verification and negative cases

Test valid/missing/incorrect identifiers, duplicates, permitted exceptions and required medical-form fields. Show required export/submission blocking and correct linkage; do not use real identity documents in demonstrations.

Artifacts after implementation

A11-identity-and-content-cases.csv; A11-validation-results.json; redacted form exhibit; form/identifier rule matrix and exception approval.

Review and ongoing operation

Registration/clinical owner witnesses patient linkage and signs form applicability. Refresh on forms, identity or mapping changes; merely displaying a code list does not prove complete medical-record compliance.

History and migration

Do not invent legacy consent times, rewrite existing clinical seals, or record mock authority responses as real acceptance.

Deployment

carejiox, carejiox_template, hhh — demo environments

Dependencies and claim limits

Technical acceptance does not replace policy approval, government decisions or clinic obligations.

Implemented control scope

Identity-readiness view flags missing/nonstandard CCCD and conflicting identity values, permits documented review exceptions and avoids blocking ordinary care. Manual VNeID assertion is distinguished from government authentication.

Remaining acceptance and external work

Verify identity ownership and approved exceptions. Custodian-approved clinical form/data-field inventory and destination-specific mandatory-content checks remain to be finalised; format checks do not establish authenticity.
