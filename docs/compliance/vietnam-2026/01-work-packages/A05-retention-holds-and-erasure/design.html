A05 — Retention, holds and erasure

Design before implementation; accepted test evidence will be generated only after a fix is in place.

Components

health_compliance, health_base, biz_tenants

Requirement coverage

EV-02-4, EV-06-1, EV-06-3

Expected application exhibit

Record-class schedule, legal hold, rejected purge and authorised disposal case.

Verification and negative cases

Use boundary-date fixtures for each approved class; refuse deletion of held/retained records and whole tenants. Prove lawful purge, attachment removal, backup expiry and restore suppression.

Artifacts after implementation

A05-retention-hold-tests.json; approved-schedule.pdf; disposal-approval.pdf; scoped-erasure-report.pdf; backup-expiry and restore-suppression results.

Review and ongoing operation

Custodian/DPO approves trigger dates, exceptions and custody. Demonstrate configuration, boundary tests and sustainable contracts; do not claim a test proves decades of elapsed storage or physical erasure of unverified media.

History and migration

Do not invent legacy consent times, rewrite existing clinical seals, or record mock authority responses as real acceptance.

Deployment

carejiox, carejiox_template, hhh — demo environments

Dependencies and claim limits

Technical acceptance does not replace policy approval, government decisions or clinic obligations.

Implemented control scope

Company-scoped retention/legal holds stop linked healthcare-record and patient deletion. Released holds retain history. Tenant closure requires an explicit custodian approval reference and no active hold. No automatic clinical destruction is enabled.

Remaining acceptance and external work

Custodian/legal team must approve retention classes, schedule, exceptions, archive custody and each destruction procedure. Final clinical notes remain locked; a reviewed statutory disposition service is future work if required.
