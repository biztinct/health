A06 — Readable complete exports and archives

Design before implementation; accepted test evidence will be generated only after a fix is in place.

Components

health_compliance, health_fhir_adapter_vn

Requirement coverage

EV-02-4, EV-06-2, EV-07-4

Expected application exhibit

Patient copy/summary and archive/export request with manifest.

Verification and negative cases

Export notes, addenda, attachments, consent and required metadata. Reconcile counts/checksums and open the archive independently of the current service; test representative format migrations.

Artifacts after implementation

A06-synthetic-export.zip; manifest.csv; schema-and-format-guide.pdf; A06-independent-read-test.pdf; signed custody/exit terms.

Review and ongoing operation

Receiving custodian witnesses readability and completeness. Refresh on export/schema/format changes; periodically rehearse retrieval and key preservation. A successful export is not a statutory national-interface submission.

History and migration

Do not invent legacy consent times, rewrite existing clinical seals, or record mock authority responses as real acceptance.

Deployment

carejiox, carejiox_template, hhh — demo environments

Dependencies and claim limits

Technical acceptance does not replace policy approval, government decisions or clinic obligations.

Implemented control scope

Restricted manager archive exports patient-linked healthcare/BHYT records, linked files, readable HTML, JSON and per-file SHA-256 manifest. Clinical integrity is verified before publication. Unsupported models and scope limits are explicit.

Remaining acceptance and external work

Reconcile the exported model inventory, fields, binary evidence, financial/chatter/external records and approved form set before calling an archive complete. PDF/PDF-A archival profiles and external records remain additional work where applicable.
