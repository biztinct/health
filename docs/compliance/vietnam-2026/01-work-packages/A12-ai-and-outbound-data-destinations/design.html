A12 — AI and outbound data destinations

Design before implementation; accepted test evidence will be generated only after a fix is in place.

Components

ai_egress, biz_bi, health_scribe, health_ai_coding

Requirement coverage

EV-01-4, EV-02-2, EV-03-2, EV-07-3

Expected application exhibit

Approved destination/purpose list and blocked-request audit event.

Verification and negative cases

Exercise every enabled AI/speech/analytics/support caller, redirects and queued work. Verify missing/withdrawn purpose blocks consent-dependent transmission and network logs show no disallowed payload left.

Artifacts after implementation

A12-data-flow-and-destinations.pdf; caller-coverage.csv; egress-denial-results.json; redacted outbound observations; provider location and processing terms.

Review and ongoing operation

DPO/security approves destinations and actual provider practices. A local label/private address or unchecked cloud flag is insufficient. Refresh on provider/model/endpoint, subprocessors or new callers.

History and migration

Do not invent legacy consent times, rewrite existing clinical seals, or record mock authority responses as real acceptance.

Deployment

carejiox, carejiox_template, hhh — demo environments

Dependencies and claim limits

Technical acceptance does not replace policy approval, government decisions or clinic obligations.

Implemented control scope

Central BI provider guard defaults unknown callers to records, binds patient AI consent to a reviewed destination, refuses remote patient text and disables redirects. Speech uses purpose/recipient gates. Free questions containing undetectable names require an approved local analytics destination.

Remaining acceptance and external work

Approve actual countries, processor terms, legal bases and destinations. Verify network controls/DNS routing and any providers outside these call paths; no complete all-integration egress claim is made.
