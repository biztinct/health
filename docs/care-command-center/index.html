Care Command Center — three design directions

One intelligent entry point for Sales to monitor and answer Zalo, VoIP24h, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, web chat, ZNS and email — knowing who is talking, what they've booked, and what to do next — and to create leads, clients and bookings or clear junk from the same surface. Three deliberately different paradigms; same cast of mock customers in each so you can compare like-for-like.

North star

Within five seconds of anything arriving on any channel, a sales person knows who this is, who they're speaking for, what's booked, why they're contacting us now and the safest next action — and can take that action without leaving the screen. Nothing is ever sent to a customer without a human tap.

Chosen direction — v3 combined POC (20 Jul 2026)

Live Wall + Messenger Zero merged, with AI as a config-gated layer

After review, the direction chosen is a zoomable single surface: the Wall for awareness & triage, which opens into the full Messenger workplace — plus a channel filter dock, claim flows everywhere, and an AI Assist configuration (Ollama-first) that gates every AI feature. With AI off, the tool runs fully on deterministic rules: identity matching, watchlist phrases, templates, junk rules, all CRM actions.

The three paradigms (original study)

Each answers "what is this tool?" differently. All three share the CMS light theme, flat colors, and the full job set (respond · identify · book · lead · client · junk · supervisor peek).

Option 1

Live Wall

"The tool is a living situation room."

Every open conversation is a breathing tile on a heat wall — size = urgency × value, edge = channel. A ringing call literally swells until answered. Click a tile and it blooms into the full merged conversation; Esc collapses it back.

  • Demand pressure is visible, not counted — hot things are bigger
  • Live now-ticker of events across every channel
  • Incoming-call context bloom: identity + bookings before you answer
  • "Everyone" toggle = inherent supervisor view (ownership rings, unclaimed zone)
Trade-off: boldest departure from list-based work; spatial layout needs discipline at 200+ live conversations (clustering, filters).

Option 2 Recommended

AI Teammate

"You don't work messages. You approve decisions."

An AI colleague has already read every channel overnight: replies drafted as real Zalo bubbles, bookings proposed with live nurse slots, identities matched with evidence, junk batched. The UI is its handover — a feed of decision cards you approve, edit or reject.

  • 80% of the day staged before you arrive; the job becomes judgment
  • Every card shows why + source events — nothing is a black box
  • Zero auto-send by design; approve-all only for the routine band
  • AI ledger: approval/edit/override rates + capability kill-switches
Trade-off: depends on draft quality; needs the full-inbox escape hatch (built in) and honest confidence honesty to earn trust.

Option 3

Messenger Zero

"The CRM dissolves into a chat app they already know."

A premium messenger — one chat per person, channels merged. The CRM melts into the thread: booking widgets with live slots materialise in-chat, identity cards slide under unknown numbers, /commands and hover actions do everything without leaving the conversation.

  • Zero learning curve — it's the Zalo muscle memory sales staff already have
  • Rich in-thread cards: booking proposals the family can confirm
  • Header flips into the client profile — context one tap away, no third pane
  • Team tab: colleagues' loads + unclaimed pool (supervisor peek)
Trade-off: chat-paced; weaker at cross-customer triage ("what's most urgent across 80 chats?") — leans on the smart sections to compensate.

Fable's recommendation

Build Option 2 (AI Teammate) as the paradigm — with Option 3's thread as its drill-down, and Option 1's wall as the team wallboard later.

These three compose rather than compete. The decision feed is the category-defining move — nothing in the Pancake / Intercom / Zendesk class inverts the inbox this way, and it attacks the real cost: agent reading-and-typing time. But every "Open thread" click needs a world-class conversation surface — that's exactly Messenger Zero, so we build it as the level below the feed. The Live Wall becomes the manager's screen (and the office TV) in a later phase, feeding off the same canonical data.

Why not 3 alone? It makes agents faster at chatting but keeps them reading everything. The leverage is in pre-staged judgment, not faster typing.
Why not 1 alone? Spectacular awareness, but awareness isn't the bottleneck for 2–5 agents — handling time is. The wall shines as a shared/supervisor surface.
De-risked path Phase A ships the merged person-thread + identity + in-thread booking (Option 3 core, no AI needed). Phase B layers the decision feed on top as drafts mature. Wall last.

Safety stance in all options: nothing auto-sends, identity never merges silently, symptom phrases only point (humans escalate), and every AI claim links to its source events.
